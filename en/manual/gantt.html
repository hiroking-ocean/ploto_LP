How to Use Gantt Chart

The Gantt Chart, the core feature of Ploto, allows you to visually manage your project's tasks and schedules. With intuitive drag-and-drop operations, you can easily change durations and move tasks.

Gantt Chart

1. Basic Operations

Here are the most fundamental operations for the Gantt Chart. You can intuitively build your schedule using mouse actions.

Adding and Switching Gantt Charts (Multi-Tab)

In Ploto, you can manage multiple Gantt charts (projects) within a single file. Click the 'Projects & Views' button on the far left sidebar, then click the '+' button next to 'Gantt Charts' to add one. Added Gantt charts appear as 'tabs' at the top of the screen. Simply click a tab to switch between them.

Adding and Deleting Tasks

Click a '+' at the right edge of the table (grid) on the left to add a new task; the details panel opens on the right. Enter the task name and press 'Apply' to confirm. The '+' in the header row adds a task at the bottom, and the '+' on a row adds it as a child of that row. To delete a task, right-click it and choose 'Delete' from the menu, or press 'Delete Task' in the details panel.

Selecting Multiple Tasks and Copying

Select several tasks with Shift+click or Ctrl+click to move, copy, duplicate, or delete them together. 'Copy' (Ctrl+C) and 'Paste' (Ctrl+V) in the right-click menu carry tasks over to another Gantt chart, including child tasks, ToDos, and assignees. Use 'Duplicate' to add a copy in place.

Configuring the Grid Header Row (Columns, Sorting, Width)

The header row of the task list (grid) on the left side of the Gantt chart is where the display settings for the table live. It is easy to overlook, but setting it up gives you a schedule that shows only the information you actually need.

1. Choose which columns to show (double-click or right-click the header row)

Double-click the header row, or right-click it and choose 'Grid Column Settings', to open the dialog. Tick the columns you want in the table from Start Date, End Date, Duration, Progress, Assignee, Kanban, Tags, ToDo, Priority and Cost (Task Name is always shown and cannot be turned off). Drag the grip on the left of each entry to reorder the columns as well. These settings are saved per view, so the same layout is waiting for you the next time you open the file.

2. Sort the rows (click a header)

Each click on a header cycles through 'ascending → descending → original order'. Six columns can be sorted: Task Name, Start Date, End Date, Duration, Progress and Priority. Sorting only changes the display — the manual order you built by dragging is kept internally, and a third click always brings it back, so you can freely compare tasks by due date or by progress.

3. Adjust the column width (drag the header border)

Drag the border between two headers to change a column's width, or double-click the border to fit the column to its content. Use it when task names are too long to read, or when you want to narrow the table to give the chart more room. Column widths are saved per view as well.

Adjusting Duration (Drag & Drop)

Drag the left or right edge of a task bar on the chart to change the start or end date. Drag the center of the bar to move the entire schedule left or right while maintaining the duration (number of days).

Setting Dependencies (Links)

You can define sequential relationships (predecessor/successor) between tasks. Drag the circular dot at the edge of a task bar and connect it to another task bar to create a link (dependency). Double-click the link line to delete it.

Updating Progress

Drag the handle at the progress boundary of a task bar left or right to change the progress rate (0% to 100%). The progress is visually represented by the darker fill inside the bar. For tasks with 'Auto-calculate progress from ToDos' turned on in the details panel, progress is calculated from the state of their ToDos.

Task Details Settings (Details, ToDos, Memo, Cost)

Click a task's row or bar to open the 'Details Panel' on the right side of the screen. This panel contains four tabs where you can edit basic items, manage ToDos, write formatted notes, and enter costs.

1. Details Tab (Basic Info Settings)

Configure the task name, start date, end date and duration, task status and progress rate, assignee, tags, task color, priority matrix, and more. Turn on 'Pin task' to keep the task from moving when other tasks move. Press 'Apply' to confirm your changes.

2. ToDos Tab (Task Breakdowns)

Enter a detailed checklist (ToDo list) associated with the task. You can assign an individual due date and assignee to each ToDo item. Each click on the circle to the left advances its state: not started → in progress → completed. For tasks on a Kanban stage, ToDos are shown separately for each stage.

3. Memo Tab (Formatted Notes)

Write detailed notes or instructions for the task. Rich-text formatting such as bold, italics, lists, and tables is supported.

4. Cost Tab (Cost Line Items)

Enter the task's costs as line items with a name, quantity, unit, and unit price. Writing day in the quantity or unit price replaces it with the task's number of days, so stretching the bar recalculates the amount (for example, 2*day). Parent tasks show the total of their child tasks. Entering costs is a Pro feature; the free version can view existing entries. You can also try it in the tutorial with the free version.

2. Advanced Features

Features designed to make your project management more detailed and robust.

Holiday & Company Off-day Settings

Register public holidays or company-specific days off from 'Configure holidays (import / edit)' in Project Settings. Registered days off are shown as a colored background on the chart. Turning on 'Working Days Calculation' excludes weekends and days off from the displayed duration without changing the dates.
*Adding and editing days off manually is free. Importing national holidays in bulk and the working days calculation are Pro features.

Task Health Line

Ploto automatically evaluates each task against today's date and shows the result as a vertical line at the left edge of its row in the table. Tasks past their end date and unfinished, or with overdue ToDos, are 'At risk' (red). Tasks with 0% progress after their start date, or with ToDos due today, are 'Warning' (orange). Tasks with no delays are 'On track' (green). This helps you find tasks that need attention before it is too late. You can turn the display on or off with 'Task Health Line' in Project Settings.

Marking Up the Chart (Fill, Icon, Comment)

The toolbar above the Gantt chart lets you write directly on the chart. 'Fill' colors the cells you drag across (painting the same color again removes it). 'Icon' places symbols such as milestones on a date. 'Comment' places a speech bubble on the chart.

Display Density and Zoom

'Display density' on the toolbar sets the row height to small, medium, or large, and is saved per Gantt view. Zoom the timeline with the zoom buttons or Ctrl+mouse wheel.

Saving and Comparing Plans (Pro)

Press 'Save current plan' under 'Plans' in the sidebar to save the current schedule under a name. Choose a saved plan with the plan comparison control on the toolbar to overlay it on the Gantt chart and see how many tasks are delayed, earlier, added, or removed.

Cost Summary (Pro)

'Cost' on the toolbar shows the total for the whole schedule, and pressing it opens a summary laid out like a quotation. Tasks stay in their hierarchy, line items can be edited on the spot, and 'Export to Excel' writes out a table with formulas. You can also check amounts in the table's 'Cost' column. The currency is set in Project Settings.

Undo / Redo

Don't worry if you make a mistake. Just like in Excel, you can use the keyboard shortcuts Ctrl + Z (Undo) and Ctrl + Y (Redo) to revert or re-apply your recent actions step-by-step.
